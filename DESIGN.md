# Rumpun design direction

Identity: a warm household ledger that treats family finances as something tended together.

Personality: grounded, reassuring, candid, and practical. Never glossy or bank-corporate.

Palette:
- Forest `#234B38` for primary actions and anchors.
- Moss `#6F8265` for supporting information.
- Clay `#A44B2B` as the single attention accent, darkened to keep white action labels above WCAG AA.
- Paper `#F7F1E7` and ink `#20251F` as the neutral ground.

Typography: Noto Serif for major titles to evoke a personal household ledger; Noto Sans for controls and financial details because compact values must remain legible.

Mood: warm paper, strong ink, restrained botanical linework, and flat surfaces.

Identity motif: one continuous branch line that joins leaf-shaped nodes, representing people and financial accounts sharing one root.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1
